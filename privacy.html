Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Millionaire Odds is designed with privacy as a default, not an afterthought. This policy explains what data we collect (very little), what we do with it (very little), and what rights you have (all the usual ones).

1. Calculator data

All calculations performed on the Millionaire Odds calculator happen entirely within your web browser. The answers you enter into the form — age, income, net worth, savings rate, and so on — are never transmitted to any server, never stored in any database, and never associated with any identifier.

When you close or navigate away from the calculator, your inputs are gone. We have no record of them. This is intentional.

2. Contact form

If you choose to contact us via the contact form, we collect:

  • Your name (as provided)
  • Your email address
  • The subject and content of your message

This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to any mailing list, share your details with third parties, or use your contact information for marketing without your explicit consent.

Contact data is retained for up to 12 months for correspondence purposes, after which it is deleted.

3. Cookies and tracking

Millionaire Odds does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or behavioural profiling technologies. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar tools that build individual user profiles.

If we ever introduce analytics in the future, they will be privacy-preserving (e.g. aggregated, anonymised page-view counts only), and this policy will be updated accordingly.

4. Third-party services

Millionaire Odds loads a font stylesheet from Google Fonts. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to download the typefaces used on this site. Google may log this request in accordance with their own privacy policy. If you prefer to avoid this, you can disable web fonts in your browser settings.

No other third-party scripts, embeds, or services are loaded on this site.

5. Data security

Because we collect so little data, the attack surface is minimal. Contact form submissions are handled over encrypted HTTPS connections. We do not store financial data, passwords, or sensitive personal information.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you. Since the only personal data we might hold is from a contact form submission, you can exercise these rights simply by emailing us at hello@millionaireodds.com with your request.

We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children

Millionaire Odds is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data via our contact form, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of Millionaire Odds after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at hello@millionaireodds.com or use the Contact page.
